# 34.21 — Home timeline shows followed actors' posts

**Phase 34 — UI polish & engagement**

## Verification

This was a verification item, not a bug fix. The home timeline (`/home`) correctly shows the followed actors' posts.

## Steps taken

1. Logged in as alice.
2. Navigated to `/directory`, followed andrew (button changed to "Unfollow").
3. Navigated to `/home` — the timeline immediately showed andrew's posts:
   - "Heeellllo" (Note)
   - "Follows alice" (Follow activity)
   - "Hello" (Note)

## Finding

The `FeedService.BuildFeedAsync` (F-14) correctly merges the followed actors' outboxes. The actor's own posts do NOT appear in the home timeline — they appear on `/profile` (the actor's outbox). This is standard ActivityPub behavior: the "followed feed" shows what you follow, not yourself.

No code change needed. Full fast suite green (1,599 passed).
